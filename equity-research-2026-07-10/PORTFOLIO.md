# PORTFOLIO MEMO — Rs 50,000 Indian Small/SME-Cap Portfolio
**Analysis date:** 2026-07-10 | **Capital:** ~Rs 50,000 | **Universe:** 21 researched names (equity-research-2026-07-10/)

---

## 0. Standing disclaimer

Small-cap and SME-board stocks carry **extreme drawdown risk (50-80% peak-to-trough is common), thin liquidity, circuit-filter traps, and near-zero institutional price discovery**. Several names in this universe round-tripped 50%+ within 12 months of listing. This memo is a research exercise on a defined universe — **not personalized investment advice**. Position sizes assume the holder can tolerate total loss of the deployed capital.

---

## 1. Selection method

1. **Verdict gate:** only Hold-or-better names, using the *adjusted* verdict where adversarial verification downgraded (Ganesh Green Bharat, Shanti Gold, Khazanchi all Hold→Avoid).
2. **Lot-size gate (hard):** SME-board lots must fit a sensible position inside Rs 50k. Any name whose minimum lot exceeds ~Rs 10k (20% cap) — let alone the whole corpus — is uninvestable here.
3. **Sizing:** conviction/risk ratio, penalized for LOW confidence and open surveillance flags; max ~20-28% per name (see §4 note on the cap); whole shares only.

**Survivors: 4 of 21.** The universe is governance-poor — the dominant failure mode across rejects is the same forensic signature: reported PAT growth with negative/collapsing OCF, ballooning receivables, small auditors, and zero institutional ownership. Target was 5-9 holdings; only 4 names legitimately survive both gates. I hold 4 rather than dilute quality — this forces weights slightly above the 20% soft cap (max 28%), which I judge a smaller risk than adding a fifth name from the Avoid pile.

**Expected high-correlation pairs:**
1. **SRM ↔ Viviana (highest):** both govt-EPC order-book stories with stretched receivables; both re-rate/de-rate on the same National Infrastructure/T&D capex news flow, budget announcements, and DISCOM payment-cycle sentiment. Treat as one ~48% "govt capex" sleeve for risk purposes.
2. **Dynacons ↔ SRM/Viviana (moderate-high):** Dynacons' clients are govt/PSU-BFSI — its order flow shares the public-capex driver even though the delivery model (IT SI) differs; its working-capital pathology (rising debtors, debt-funded growth) is the same factor.
3. **Ksolves ↔ Dynacons (moderate):** both "IT" but different demand engines (US private clients vs domestic PSU tenders) — sector label overlap overstates true correlation; still, both sell off together in any small-cap-IT de-rating.
4. **All four (dominant common factor):** small-cap liquidity beta. In a broad risk-off, correlations converge toward 1 regardless of fundamentals — expect the whole book to draw down together. Diversification here is genuine at the *business-driver* level but weak at the *liquidity/flow* level. A quantitative price-based matrix is being computed separately and should be checked against this qualitative read (thin-float price series will understate true co-movement).

## 6. Monitoring triggers (pre-registered, per name)

**Portfolio-wide (any name):** promoter pledge appears; auditor resignation/qualification; SEBI enforcement order; ASM/GSM escalation; OCF/PAT deteriorating vs the entry-thesis figure for 2 consecutive halves → exit or halve.

- **SRM:** (1) any escalation of the Apr-2026 SEBI RPT warning into adjudication → exit; (2) FY27 H1 OCF turns materially negative again (payables-stretch unwind) → halve; (3) new unapproved RPTs with Rajinder/RSB JVs → exit; (4) J&K order-book share rising again → review.
- **Ksolves:** (1) FY27 PAT still flat while employee cost >52% of revenue → exit (thesis is margin stabilization); (2) top-10 client concentration >60% or a marquee client loss → halve; (3) any receivables spike beyond revenue growth (auditor's recurring KAM) → review.
- **Dynacons:** (1) **ASM escalation to Stage II+ or GSM → exit immediately** (tightest trigger in book); (2) borrowings grow another >30% YoY or D/E >1.0 → exit; (3) OCF/PAT conversion <40% for FY27 → halve; (4) debtor days >170 → review.
- **Viviana:** (1) FY27 OCF/PAT <25% → **exit — this is the load-bearing kill trigger**; the entire hold is conditional on cash conversion appearing; (2) BESS/transformer capex overruns or further 12%-coupon debt → exit; (3) Gujarat MoU converts to firm orders → positive re-size review; (4) debtor days >260 → exit.

**Review cadence:** quarterly results + a standing check of ASM/GSM lists and BSE/NSE filings for all four names.
