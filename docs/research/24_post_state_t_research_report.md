## Chapter 1 — What Was Killed, Exactly

### 1.1 The object and its pre-registered claim

State T was the programme's Layer-2 (regime-shift detection) flagship: the hypothesis that an extended
deviation from equilibrium μ* **loses momentum and stabilizes before reverting**, leaving a measurable
*pre-reversion morphology*. Doc 11 operationalized this — deliberately as an **observational existence
question, never a detector** — on the causal Kalman innovation stream `epsilon_kalman`. The
pre-registered signature, fixed before any real data was touched, was that high-|z| causal pre-windows
(anchored by a symmetric multi-θ partition, θ∈{1.0, 1.5, 2.0}, W=30) would show, relative to a matched
null, all three of:

- **D1 innovation variance ↓** (residual stabilization),
- **D2 continuation persistence ACF(1) ↓** (deviation losing momentum),
- **D3 directional efficiency ↓** (price chops rather than continues).

The "T-signature" was defined as **all three descriptors negative**. Critically, the claim was about a
*distribution*, never a bar — the gating test for legality was "does the output's meaning depend on
*when* you read it?" If yes → timing/hazard → frozen. This kept the existence question on the legal
side of the State-T detection freeze.

### 1.2 The method was validated before it was trusted

Phase 3 established that the machinery was **not blind** (a precondition for a credible negative). On
synthetic ground truth it produced **no false positive** on ordinary OU or random-walk mean reversion
(all |d| ≤ 0.15 across θ and all three descriptors), and it fired **loudly and in the correct
direction** on a clean stabilize-then-revert positive control (innov_var d ≈ −1.9, ACF1 d ≈ −2.1 to
−2.5, dir_eff d ≈ −0.9). A first positive-control attempt was *rejected and recorded* (a mis-built
two-regime OU whose high-|z| windows were dominated by a trendy approach) — a METHODOLOGY finding, not
a method defect, preserved without revisionism. **Confidence in the method: MEDIUM-HIGH; confidence
that State T exists: UNTESTED.** This is the discipline that made the subsequent kill interpretable
rather than merely null.

### 1.3 The pre-registered kill condition, and why it fired

The decisive instrument was **pre-committed**: the HDFC–ICICI pair spread, the *canonical
mean-reversion habitat*. The committed decision rule (doc 11 Phase 5): C-signature in the pair spread →
thesis strengthens; directional continuation like the rest of the cohort → **kill condition met** (the
single best habitat failed). The rule fired.

Across **12 instruments / 5+ habitats / 2–3 resolutions / 2 comparison methods / 3 θ**, `dir_eff` was
**positive in every one of the 12** — high-deviation windows were *more* directionally efficient
(continuation), the exact opposite of the pre-registered stabilization. The within-instrument
confound-controlled comparison (more adversarial than the vs-OU read) reproduced the sign, so the
result was not a null-calibration artifact. The decisive pair spread showed directional continuation
like everything else.

A null adversary then reproduced the **entire** 12-instrument result with **no State T at all**:
**selection-on-deviation**. Anchoring on |z| ≥ θ samples paths that *arrived at an extreme by
directional travel*; those pre-windows are directionally efficient *by construction*. The magnitude is
inflated by vs-OU comparison, but the **sign** is set by real path shape. No residual requires State T.

> **Problem class: STRUCTURAL** (the phenomenon as formulated is absent), explicitly **not**
> MEASUREMENT — the positive control rules out the trailing-window-geometry artifact, because the same

published evidence; it would have been the *surprise* had State T survived.

It **licensed** the move to Arm 0 (provenance) and Arm A (habitat discovery) — the foundation. It
**forbade** any drift into a refined timing object, and it set the next high-information question with

## Chapter 3 — Arm 0: The Provenance Audit

### 3.1 Why provenance is upstream of every statistic

Every cross-instrument conclusion the programme could draw rested on instruments whose trustworthiness
was *unverified*. The first-principles thesis (doc 12 Arm 0): **you cannot characterise "which markets
mean-revert" on instruments that may be stationary by lookahead.** A spread built with a *full-sample*
hedge ratio is **stationary by hindsight construction** — mechanically indistinguishable from genuine
mean reversion, and impossible to detect after the fact from a single precomputed column. Provenance is
the gate.

The disposition rules were pre-committed *before* the audit so the whitelist could not be post-hoc:
**TRUSTED** (raw single-asset or confirmed causal/rolling β, ≥750 daily-equivalent bars, monotone
dates, documented units); **PROVISIONAL** (raw, adequate, fixable hygiene flag); **CONTAMINATED**
(spread with full-sample or unverifiable β → excluded from any habitat verdict); **UNUSABLE** (below
the power floor, corrupted, or degenerate). **Contamination is the default; trust is earned.**

### 3.2 The finding: 0 of 8 admissible

The audit returned a **binding empirical fact**: every deployment-domain "spread" on disk was a
**precomputed single `close` column — the legs were discarded.** With the legs gone, the hedge ratio's
causality is unverifiable from the file, and a full-sample β cannot be distinguished from honest
reversion. Consequently **0 of 8 deployment-domain spreads survived to the Arm-A whitelist; all 8 were
CONTAMINATED by default.** (Problem class: MEASUREMENT/DATA + STRUCTURAL.) The audit *concluding that
most of the cohort was unusable was itself the critical finding*, not a failure of the audit.

### 3.3 The old cohort fails; a deep cohort arrives

The provenance verdict converted the binding bottleneck from theory to data. The resolution was a
**deep cohort of 46 TRUSTED raw legs** — individual, positive-price, causally-rollable instrument legs
across energy, metals, agriculture, FX, and equities — from which **7 spreads are constructible** under
a causal protocol. For the first time the programme had instruments whose causality could be **proven
by construction** rather than assumed. (CLAUDE.md §11.7 records this as the data-phase transition: the
bottleneck is no longer data, but disciplined evidence generation.)

## Chapter 4 — The Canonical Spread Protocol (Doc 13)

### 4.1 The single governing question

Doc 13 is the permanent generalization of the Arm-0 verdict into a **construction-legality law**. It
produces no scores, signals, or detectors — it governs data construction only, upstream of every
reversion statistic. Its governing question, applied to every derived value at every bar:

> *Could this exact value have existed, computed only from information available at time t, at time t?*

If the answer is "no" or "cannot be shown" → **CONTAMINATED**, and the value may not feed any habitat
verdict. The law has six pillars, all FROZEN.

### 4.2 The frozen pillars

- **Hedge-ratio ontology (HR-1…HR-5).** Ordered from safest to most assumption-laden:
  **β=1 definitional** (calendars, same-underlying — *zero* hedge-ratio lookahead; a fitted β is
  *forbidden* where 1:1 is definitional, HR-1); **structural/economic β** (crack/crush ratios, DV01;
  HR-5); **rolling-β** on a trailing window (equity pairs; the standard remedy vs full-sample, HR-2);
  **rolling-cointegration** (Engle–Granger for k=2, Johansen for k≥3; causal windows only, HR-3); and
  **Kalman/state-space dynamic β** — **DEFERRED, not authorized for v0** (HR-4). A **full-sample /
  whole-sample β of any kind is PERMANENTLY FORBIDDEN as evidence** — it *is* the Arm-0 contamination.
- **Causality firewall (C-1…C-6).** Trailing-window estimation only; warmup masking (never back-fill);
  **lag the β** (estimate on ≤ t−1, apply at t — even a one-bar contemporaneous fit is lookahead,
  the load-bearing C-3); fixed pre-registered rebalance cadence; timestamp (not row-index)
  synchronization; **no future normalization** (trailing, additive moments only).
- **Alignment (AL-1…AL-3).** Inner-join on synchronized UTC timestamps is the mandated default; a
  high forward-fill fraction marks a series CONTAMINATED (fabricated stillness mimics MR); back-fill
  and interpolation are forbidden, always.
- **Negative prices (NP-1…NP-3).** A spread that can cross zero is a **level series**: its dynamics are
  **first differences `ΔS = S_t − S_{t−1}`**, never log or simple returns (undefined through zero).
  Return-space statistics belong on the **legs**, not the spread — which is *why* Arm A's return-space
  whitelist is single-name positive instruments.
- **Roll adjustment (RA-1…RA-3).** Additive (Panama) back-adjustment is **forbidden as a spread leg**
  (it injects drift that manufactures or masks MR); ratio adjustment is forbidden (rescaled legs are
  not in comparable price units); the preferred construction builds the spread from actual contracts on
  a causal roll calendar.
- **Spread OHLC legality (OHLC-1…OHLC-5).** Open and Close are timestamp-synchronized and **TRUSTED**;
  naïve **High/Low formed from per-leg extrema are counterfactual states that never existed** and are
  **FORBIDDEN** unless reconstructed from synchronized intrabar leg paths. Every spread statistic
  defaults to Close.

### 4.3 The standing treatment

A constructed spread is admissible as evidence only if **all** pillars hold; otherwise it is
CONTAMINATED. This is the permanent statement of the Arm-0 lesson: **the bottleneck is provenance, not
theory.** Confidence in the protocol: **MEDIUM-HIGH** (first-principles consequences of the lookahead
constitution, independently corroborated for the OHLC law by CQG and by in-repo invalid-bar counts).

## Chapter 5 — The Rolling-OLS-β Manufactured-VR Finding (Arm A v1, Doc 19)

### 5.1 The setup

Arm A v1 executed the first verdict-capable deployment-domain MR read in the programme: 7 spreads built
from the trusted legs under the doc-13 protocol (inner-join, level differences, Open/Close, causal
**lagged rolling-OLS β** on a W=60 window), tested with the level-difference variance ratio
`VR(q) = Var(S_t − S_{t−q}) / (q·Var(ΔS))`, q∈{2,5,10,20}, against RW, GARCH(1,1), and OU surrogates,
multiplicity-corrected. Every spread passed a **future-injection bit-identity** causal acceptance test;
the engine was ground-truth validated (1.7% corrected FPR, 100% power at φ∈{0.95,0.9,0.8}).

### 5.2 The defect: a causal estimator that manufactures its own answer

The headline result was **INCONCLUSIVE — and the pre-registered test was demonstrably
construction-defective.** Decompose the frozen increment:

```
ΔS_t = (ΔA_t − β_{t−1}·ΔB_t)  −  (β_{t−1} − β_{t−2})·B_{t−1}
                                  └──────────── β-update-noise × a large trending level ────────────┘
```

The second term — the change in the hedge ratio times the *level* of a trending leg — was measured
(independently by two reviewers) at **82–97% of Var(ΔS)** on the failing spreads, with ACF ≈ 0.81. It
injects a smooth, persistent component that drives **VR ≫ 1** (spurious super-diffusion). This was
**proven on synthetic ground truth**: for `A = 3·B + OU` (the true spread *is* OU, true VR < 1), the
true-β VR was [0.97, 0.91, 0.81, 0.65], but **rolling-β at W=60 fabricated VR = [1.81, 3.68, 5.46,
6.23]** — and ablating the β-update term recovered the true VR < 1.

This is the **doc-07 lag-illusion / doc-13 HR-4 mechanism at the hedge-ratio level**: a time-varying β
allowed to chase the spread absorbs the deviation into β and manufactures apparent structure. Doc 13
had transferred this claim from the μ* level as a *conjecture*; doc 19 **demonstrated it at the β
level** (HR-4 STRENGTHENED).

### A.2 Core formulae

**Level-difference variance ratio** (the one core statistic that escapes the smoother trap, valid on
sign-crossing levels):
```
VR(q) = Var(S_t − S_{t−q}) / ( q · Var(S_t − S_{t−1}) )
```
`VR < 1` ⇒ sub-diffusion (mean-reverting direction); `VR ≈ 1` ⇒ martingale; `VR > 1` ⇒ super-diffusion
(trending). Always read **surrogate-relative** (real − matched null under identical extraction), never
against a fixed 1.0.

**The β-update-noise decomposition** (why rolling-OLS-β-on-levels manufactures VR; doc 19):
```
ΔS_t = (ΔA_t − β_{t−1}·ΔB_t)  −  (β_{t−1} − β_{t−2})·B_{t−1}
```
The second term — hedge-ratio change × a trending leg *level* — was 82–97% of `Var(ΔS)` on failing
spreads and drives VR ≫ 1. A constant β (β=1) removes it identically.

**Pooled persistence statistic** (doc 22/23, trader-persistence): with `z_w` standardized in-window,
```
pooled mean-z = (1/W) Σ_w z_w ,   tested one-sided vs the RW template.
```
NG yearly: pooled mean-z = −0.627, t = −4.37, p ≈ 0.0002.

**Costed trade hurdle** (the economic gate that defines a non-finding):
```
net per trade = gross reversion capture − round-trip cost ;   deployable ⇒ E[net] > 0.
```
NG: gross +0.0004 vs cost 0.003 ⇒ net −0.0026 ⇒ **uneconomic**.

### A.3 Methods (named to close them off)

- **Surrogate ensemble** — RW, GARCH(1,1), MA(1)-microstructure-noise, OU; identical length / mask / VR
  extraction so construction bias cancels; min-VR at the 5th percentile, multiplicity-corrected.
- **Causal firewall acceptance test** — detonate a future leg bar; every earlier β and spread value must
  be bit-identical.
- **selection-on-deviation** — the complete null for State T: anchoring on |z| ≥ θ samples directionally-
  efficient paths by construction.
- **Excluded by name:** full-sample / whole-sample β (forbidden as evidence); rolling-OLS-β-on-levels
  (inadmissible, manufactures VR); naïve spread High/Low (counterfactual); self-ranked MRScore (inverts);
  per-bar habitat/timing objects (State-T-adjacent, frozen).

### A.4 Key concepts

- **Smoother-manufactured reversion** — a faster-adapting fit (μ* or a chasing β) mechanically produces a
  more-reverting, lower-variance residual; "did price come back?" is contaminated. Discriminate via
  **equilibrium stability**.
- **Three wins** — research (apparatus works) · empirical (signal real) · deployment (clears costs).
  Never conflated.
- **Zombie prohibition** — a killed idea (State T, rolling-OLS-β, MRScore-as-signal, predictive
  transition) reopens only by surfacing why it failed, what new evidence exists, why prior objections no
  longer bind, and which trigger fired.
