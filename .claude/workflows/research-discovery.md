# Research Discovery Workflow

Goal:
Continuously discover high-signal ideas relevant to AMR, deployable mean reversion, market structure, equilibrium dynamics, residual behavior, spread construction, and trader-relevant alpha.

Primary Objective:

> find ideas worth testing.

Not interesting ideas.

Not elegant ideas.

Ideas that survive strong falsification pressure and could plausibly generate deployable alpha.

---

## Research Sources

Search broadly.

Domains:

### Core Quant / Finance
- SSRN

---

## Process

### Stage 1 — Broad Search (Haiku / Sonnet)

Search many candidate papers.

Goal:

> maximize breadth.

Do NOT overthink.

Gather candidate concepts.

---

### Stage 2 — Deep Examination (Opus)

For shortlisted ideas:

Determine:

1. What is the actual claim?
2. What is the economic meaning?
3. Why might this work?
4. Why might this fail?
5. Is it genuinely novel relative to AMR?
6. Does this overlap existing hypotheses?
7. Would a trader care?
8. Could this realistically survive falsification?

---

## Hard Constraints

Do NOT recommend:

- ideas already rejected
- State T-like fragile elegance
- purely theoretical constructs
- non-falsifiable ideas
- ideas impossible to operationalize
- obvious textbook concepts

Strong skepticism required.

---

## Ranking Criteria

Rank by:

1. Statistical plausibility
2. Logical coherence
3. Novelty
4. Trader relevance
5. Implementability
6. Orthogonality to current thinking
7. Potential alpha contribution

---

## Required Output

Update:

`docs/research/RESEARCH_CANDIDATES.md`

Structure:

### High Priority
Worth immediate assimilation.

### Probationary
Interesting but uncertain.

### Weak
Low EV.

### Reject
Do not revisit.

For each idea include:

- short explanation
- source
- why it matters
- strongest criticism
- AMR compatibility
- recommended next step

Optimize for:

> signal density, not quantity.
