# Hypothesis Assimilation Workflow

Goal:
Convert research ideas into AMR-compatible, falsifiable hypotheses.

Mission:

> translate insight into testable structure.

Research discovery is NOT implementation.

This workflow determines:

> whether an idea deserves admission into the research programme.

---

## Step 1 — Domain Fit

Understand current AMR state.

Read selectively:

- PROJECT_STATE.md
- HYPOTHESIS_REGISTRY.md
- current Arm findings
- frozen assumptions
- CLAUDE.md doctrine

Question:

Where does this idea belong?

Potential destinations:

- Arm A
- residual ecology
- equilibrium modeling
- spread ontology
- substrate dynamics
- trader filter layer
- future research arm

Reject forced fits.

---

## Step 2 — Quant Formalization

Determine:

1. What exactly is the claim?
2. What mathematical form could represent it?
3. What assumptions are required?
4. What data is required?
5. What failure conditions exist?

Question:

> can this become a serious quantitative object?

---

## Step 3 — Statistical Audit

Determine:

- testability
- null construction
- DGP assumptions
- multiplicity concerns
- identifiability
- robustness requirements
- rolling-local implications

Question:

> can this be falsified honestly?

---

## Step 4 — Adversarial Review

Attack the proposal.

Search for:

- circularity
- hidden DOF
- ontology mismatch
- confounding
- overfit risk
- pseudo-predictiveness
- fragile assumptions

Brutal skepticism required.

---

## Step 5 — Trader / PM Review

Question:

> would anyone care?

Evaluate:

- deployability
- holding horizon
- market realism
- no-trade environments
- edge plausibility

---

## Required Output

Update:

`docs/research/HYPOTHESIS_REGISTRY.md`

Also create:

`docs/research/HYPOTHESIS_PROPOSAL.md`

Include:

### Claim
### Mathematical Form
### Assumptions
### Falsification Route
### Empirical Route
### Risks
### Integration Scope

Status:

- ACTIVE
- PROBATIONARY
- DEFERRED
- REJECTED

No vague ideas survive this workflow.
