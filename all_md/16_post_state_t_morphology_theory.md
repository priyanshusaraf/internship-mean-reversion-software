## 0. What this is, and the hard guardrails it respects

The empirical object to be *explained* (not re-derived): around large **causal** deviations from equilibrium (μ\*), real price paths exhibit directional **CONTINUATION** — higher directional efficiency and (mostly) positive innovation ACF1 inside high-|z| windows — across **12 instruments / 5+ habitats / 2–3 resolutions / 2 comparison methods / 3 θ**, including the pre-registered decisive HDFC–ICICI pair spread (doc 11 Phases 4/4b/5; doc 12 §1). The earlier hypothesis — pre-window *stabilization-then-reversion* (innov_var↓ / acf1↓ / dir_eff↓) — is **FALSIFIED-IN-FORM / KILLED** (STRUCTURAL, ~5–10% residual confidence).

This document is a **menu of candidate economic mechanisms** that *could* generate continuation-around-large-deviation, each with (a) precise mechanism, (b) economic intuition, (c) the observable causal signature that would separate it from the null and from its rivals, (d) a cheap, causal, pre-registerable *future* falsification hook. It is **mechanism mapping only**.

**Hard guardrails (binding, from the task + CLAUDE.md §4/§10 + doc 11 §6):**

- **NO State-T resurrection.** None of these mechanisms is a re-skin of "pre-window stabilization." They explain the *surviving, opposite-signed* finding (continuation), not the killed one. Zombie prohibition applies: a mechanism that reduces to the killed morphology is rejected on sight.
- **NO new theory proposal, NO commitment.** A menu is not a hypothesis. Promotion of any single mechanism to a tested hypothesis is a *separate*, independently pre-registered, alignment-reviewed act (doc 11 Phase-5 reopen rule; doc 12 §3 "Arm B only-if-pre-registered").
- **NO detector / score / timing / per-bar object.** Every "signature" below is a *corpus-level, time-symmetric, distributional* contrast vs a matched surrogate — never a per-bar quantity whose meaning depends on *when* you read it (doc 11 §1 gating test). Nothing here implies "continuation is happening now."
- **The NULL is the reference frame, not a competitor to be dismissed.** Selection-on-deviation (§1) reproduced the *entire* 12-instrument result with zero new mechanism (doc 11 Phase 5 null-adversary). Every other mechanism in this map must be scored by **how cleanly it separates from that null** — i.e., what it predicts that selection-on-deviation alone does *not*. A mechanism with no surrogate-exceeding signature is **not admissible** as a distinct explanation (doc 12 §4 Arm B: "count only real morphology that exceeds surrogate frequency").
- **Etiology constraint.** Several mechanisms (inventory, forced positioning, liquidity vacuum) are flow/microstructure objects. The data audit + red-team verdict is that real-time causal etiology identification is **unsolved and the required data is absent** on the current cohort (OHLC-only, most spreads volume-less; doc 12 §2 Arm D, §4). Their falsification hooks are therefore **data-gated** and explicitly flagged as such — specified for the *future*, not runnable now.

**Load-bearing inherited rule (doc 12 §2; doc 08 §7):** residual reversion / residual-ACF / half-life are **smoother-manufactured** (a pure RW's EMA residual shows ACF≈0.88, half-life≈6). The only validated discriminator is **equilibrium stability** ("did μ\* stay put?"), and **return-space variance-ratio** is the one core statistic that escapes the smoother trap. Every falsification hook below is therefore constructed in return/level-difference space or as a surrogate-relative distributional contrast — never as "did the residual revert?"

## 1. THE NULL — Selection-on-deviation (+ ordinary momentum/repricing)

**This is the reference frame. Read it first; everything else is scored against it.**

**(a) Mechanism.** Conditioning a window on **|z| ≥ θ** is conditioning on the *endpoint* of the path being extreme. Among all paths, those that *reach* a large deviation by bar t are disproportionately the ones that travelled there **directionally** (monotone, high net/path ratio) rather than by a high-variance round trip. Anchoring on the deviation therefore **samples directionally-efficient paths by construction** — the *sign* of high dir_eff is set by the selection, before any economics. Layered on top is **ordinary momentum / gradual repricing** (Jegadeesh–Titman 1993 intermediate continuation; George–Hwang 2004 — reversals do *not* occur at 52-week-high extremes), which supplies real positive short-lag autocorrelation independent of the selection. Formally this is the **Lo–MacKinlay (1990) cross-autocovariance selection trap**: contrarian/extreme-conditioned samples inherit lead–lag and selection structure, not a distinct state.

**(b) Why continuation around large deviations.** No mechanism is required. Extreme endpoints are *survivorship of directional travel*; the conditional expectation of dir_eff is mechanically elevated; momentum adds a small genuine drift. The window-averaged descriptor over [t−W+1, t] anchored at peak |z| is **dominated by the directional run-up** (doc 11 §4 non-conclusion 1). This is *exactly* what a matched OU/RW/GARCH surrogate produces under identical |z| anchoring — and indeed the synthetic battery (doc 11 Phase 3) showed OU-real vs OU-null and RW-real vs RW-null all |d| ≤ 0.15: the machinery manufactures *no* extra morphology beyond selection.

**(c) Signature that separates it from the others (the null's fingerprint).** The null predicts that **real morphology equals matched-surrogate morphology under identical anchoring** — the *whole* effect is reproduced by an OU/RW/GARCH process with no inventory, no diffusion, no positioning, no extrapolation. Its magnitude scales with θ (more extreme → more selected → more directional) and with sample autocorrelation, but it carries **no cross-sectional, volume, flow, or post-peak asymmetry** beyond what the surrogate has. Critically: under the null, the continuation is **path-shape selection**, so it is *symmetric in time-direction up to the autocorrelation* and shows **no excess at the deviation relative to a same-|z| surrogate**.

**(d) Falsification hook (this is the *anti*-hook — every other mechanism must beat it).** **Matched-surrogate subtraction.** Pre-register, per *trusted* instrument (Arm-0 whitelist), a matched OU/RW/GARCH surrogate calibrated to that instrument's return-diff variance and autocorrelation; run the *identical* |z|-anchored extraction on real and surrogate; report the distributional gap (real − surrogate) on dir_eff, ACF1, and any mechanism-specific descriptor. **If real ≈ surrogate at all θ and all horizons → the null is sufficient and no further mechanism is admissible** (doc 12 §4 Arm B kill). Every mechanism below is "admissible" only to the extent it predicts a *specific, signed* real-minus-surrogate residual the null cannot.

*Status vs the others:* **Cannot be separated by morphology alone** — it is the morphology. It is separated only by what it *lacks*: cross-sectional structure, flow conditioning, post-peak asymmetry, and reversal at long horizon. Ranking everything below = ranking by how cleanly each supplies one of those.

## 2. Inventory pressure / risk-transfer

**(a) Mechanism.** A risk-averse intermediary absorbing one-sided, asynchronously-arriving order flow moves price *away* from fundamental value to be compensated for holding non-zero inventory, then lets price **mean-revert as inventory is worked off** (Hendershott–Menkveld 2014, *Price Pressures*, JFE — NYSE specialist data: 0.49% average price pressure, **half-life ≈ 0.92 days**, larger/slower in small stocks). Grossman–Miller (1988) market-making and Campbell–Grossman–Wang (1993) are the same family: uninformed selling → temporary concession → reversal as compensation to liquidity providers.

**(b) Why continuation around large deviations.** Two faces. *During* absorption (the build phase) price pushes **directionally** in the flow's direction — a large deviation that is *still being fed* by inventory accumulation looks like continuation. The **reversion** is the *aftermath* (post-peak), which the killed pre-window geometry averaged away (doc 11 §4 non-conclusion 1) — so on the **pre-window** descriptor this mechanism *also* reads as continuation, but for a transitory reason. This is the mechanism doc 12 §2 calls the best-grounded principle in the programme (inventory/flow → reverts).

**(c) Separating signature vs the null & rivals.** The discriminator the null *cannot* fake is **post-peak transitory reversal on a microstructure timescale (sub-day to a few days), conditioned on flow imbalance, NOT on the deviation itself.** Specifically: (i) continuation that **reverses** within ~1 trading day (Hendershott–Menkveld half-life), distinguishing it from information momentum (§3, which does *not* reverse — George–Hwang) and from permanent repricing; (ii) reversal magnitude **increasing in order-flow imbalance / decreasing in liquidity depth** (small/illiquid instruments slower & larger), a cross-sectional gradient the selection null has no access to; (iii) the deviation is **uninformed** (no contemporaneous fundamental news / earnings / macro print). Versus selection-on-deviation: the null predicts dir_eff↑ but **no systematic post-peak reversal beyond surrogate**; inventory predicts a *signed, flow-graded, fast* post-peak reversal.

**(d) Future falsification hook (cheap, causal, pre-registerable — DATA-GATED).** On a **volume-bearing, flow-driven** instrument (doc 12 §4 notes a bounded volume→reversal probe is possible on the few volume-bearing series), pre-register: partition causal |z|-peaks by *contemporaneous signed volume / OFI tercile*; measure the **forward [t, t+h] level-difference reversal** for small h (intraday → ≤3 days). **Prediction:** reversal is monotone increasing in the absolute flow imbalance and concentrated at short h, and **absent** in a matched OU/GARCH surrogate (which has no flow channel). **Falsified if** reversal is flat across flow terciles or matched by the surrogate. *Gate:* requires signed volume / OFI — unavailable on most current spreads (doc 12 Arm D); spec'd, not runnable now. A Campbell–Grossman–Wang-style **volume → next-period autocorrelation-decline** check is the minimal observational stub on the volume-bearing subset.

## 3. Information momentum / gradual diffusion (underreaction)

**(a) Mechanism.** Information diffuses **gradually** across a heterogeneous population; "newswatchers" each see part of the signal and cannot extract others' information from price, so price **underreacts** and drifts toward the new fundamental over days–weeks; momentum traders then partially arbitrage and (eventually, at longer horizon) overshoot (Hong–Stein 1999, *Unified Theory*, JF). Jegadeesh–Titman (1993) is the reduced-form fact (3–12-month continuation); Daniel–Hirshleifer–Subrahmanyam (1998) is the overconfidence/self-attribution variant producing short-lag positive autocorrelation (momentum) then long-lag reversal.

**(b) Why continuation around large deviations.** A large deviation that coincides with **genuine news** is the *beginning* of a repricing, not a temporary dislocation: under gradual diffusion the price has not yet fully incorporated the signal, so the path **continues in the same direction** as the remaining information arrives. Crucially, this continuation is toward a **new equilibrium** — it is *permanent repricing*, the opposite transitory-status from inventory (§2). This is precisely George–Hwang (2004): returns forecast by nearness-to-extreme **do not reverse** — short-term momentum and long-term reversal are *separate* phenomena.

**(c) Separating signature vs the null & rivals.** The fingerprint the null lacks: continuation that is **conditioned on information events** and is **permanent** (μ\* itself moves — equilibrium does *not* stay put). Discriminators: (i) continuation **correlated with public-information arrival** (earnings, macro, supply shocks) rather than with pure |z|; (ii) **no short-horizon reversal** (separating it from inventory §2) — the deviation persists as a level shift; (iii) **equilibrium instability** — under the validated discriminator (doc 12 §2), μ\* *fails to stay put*, distinguishing genuine repricing from any transitory mechanism whose μ\* is stable. Versus the null: selection-on-deviation gives continuation with a *stationary* μ\* and no event-conditioning; information momentum gives continuation with a *broken* μ\* tied to news.

**(d) Future falsification hook (cheap, causal, pre-registerable — partially data-gated).** Pre-register an **equilibrium-stability split**: at causal |z|-peaks, classify each by whether μ\* (Kalman/EMA level) **structurally shifts** over the forward window (a return-space, smoother-trap-safe break test on the *level*, not a residual-reversion test) vs returns to its prior level. **Prediction:** the information-momentum subset shows continuation **with** a permanent μ\* shift and **no** [t, t+h] reversal; it is *over-represented around dated news* (where a news calendar exists). **Falsified if** continuation episodes systematically revert to the prior μ\* (→ transitory, favors §2) or show no μ\*-break excess vs surrogate. *Gate:* the μ\*-break test is runnable on the trusted cohort now (it is equilibrium-stability, already the licensed discriminator); the *news-conditioning* leg needs an event calendar (data-gated for spreads).

## 4. Forced positioning / margin & loss spirals (deleveraging)

**(a) Mechanism.** A self-reinforcing **funding–liquidity loop**: an initial adverse move raises margins / hits risk limits → constrained traders are **forced to liquidate** in the direction of the move → price falls further → more margin calls (Brunnermeier–Pedersen 2009, *Market Liquidity and Funding Liquidity*, RFS — destabilizing margins, "loss spirals" and "margin spirals"). The flow-driven-fire-sale cousin: Coval–Stafford 2007 (mutual-fund outflow-forced sales create predictable price pressure with **subsequent reversal**; liquidity providers earn the spread).

**(b) Why continuation around large deviations.** During the spiral, selling **begets selling** — the deviation *accelerates* and is maximally directional (continuation is the spiral's signature). Unlike slow inventory (§2), the spiral is **mechanically amplifying** and **non-fundamental**, so it tends to **overshoot** and then **reverse** once forced selling exhausts and liquidity providers step in (Coval–Stafford reversal). It is a *transitory* mechanism, like inventory, but with a distinct **convex/accelerating** build and **commonality across co-held assets**.

**(c) Separating signature vs the null & rivals.** Fingerprints the null lacks: (i) **acceleration / convexity** of the deviation (the path's directional efficiency *and* its second difference rise together — a spiral, not a drift), separating it from the linear-ish drift of information momentum; (ii) **cross-sectional commonality** — co-held / co-margined instruments deviate *together* (Brunnermeier–Pedersen commonality; Coval–Stafford overlapping holdings), a portfolio-level signature pure selection and single-asset momentum cannot produce; (iii) **coincidence with funding-stress / volatility-spike / margin-change windows**; (iv) **predictable post-exhaustion reversal** (Coval–Stafford), separating it from permanent repricing (§3). Versus inventory (§2): same transitory reversal, but spiral adds *acceleration + cross-asset commonality + funding conditioning*.

**(d) Future falsification hook (cheap, causal, pre-registerable — DATA-GATED).** Pre-register a **cross-sectional co-deviation** test: on a set of plausibly co-held/co-margined instruments, measure whether large causal |z|-deviations **cluster contemporaneously** across the set beyond what independent matched surrogates produce, and whether clustered-deviation episodes show **convex acceleration** + **post-exhaustion reversal**. **Prediction:** spiral episodes are *synchronized, convex, and revert*; idiosyncratic episodes are not. **Falsified if** deviations are independent across the set / linear / non-reverting. *Gate:* needs OI / positioning / margin or funding-stress proxies (doc 12 Arm D unlock = "signed order flow OR OI/positioning on a flow-driven instrument"); the *co-deviation clustering* leg is partially runnable on a multi-instrument trusted panel without flow data (clustering + convexity are price-only), the *funding-conditioning* leg is not.

## 5. Liquidity vacuum / fragmentation (depth depletion)

**(a) Mechanism.** Around a large move, resting **order-book depth evaporates** faster than it replenishes; liquidity providers widen/withdraw, so a given order size produces an outsized, **self-propagating** price impact until liquidity returns (Kirilenko–Kyle–Samadi–Tuzun 2017, *The Flash Crash*, JF — HFT intermediary inventories too small to absorb a large sell program; their responses *exacerbated* the move). This is a *temporary depth* failure, distinct from a *funding* failure (§4) and a *fundamental* repricing (§3).

**(b) Why continuation around large deviations.** When depth is thin, price "**gaps**" along the direction of pressure — each marketable order walks a depleted book — producing a short, sharp, **highly directional** excursion (extreme dir_eff). Because the dislocation is *liquidity*, not information, it is **transitory** and snaps back once depth replenishes (the canonical flash-crash V-shape). The continuation is an *artifact of microstructure*, sharing the transitory-status of §2/§4 but on the **fastest** timescale and tied to **depth**, not flow or funding.

**(c) Separating signature vs the null & rivals.** Fingerprints: (i) **fastest timescale + sharpest reversal** (intraday V), separating it from the ~1-day inventory half-life (§2) and the multi-day spiral (§4); (ii) **spread-widening / depth-collapse coincident with the move** (a microstructure observable, absent in any price-only surrogate); (iii) **discontinuity / gappiness** of the path (jumps, not diffusion) — a high realized-quadratic-variation-in-few-bars signature; (iv) **near-complete retracement** (transitory ≈ 100% reversal), versus partial reversal for inventory and *zero* for repricing. Versus the null: selection gives smooth directional run-ups; a vacuum gives **gappy, retracing spikes** with depth signatures the surrogate cannot have.

**(d) Future falsification hook (cheap, causal, pre-registerable — HEAVILY DATA-GATED).** Pre-register, on an instrument with **L2 depth / spread** data, a partition of causal |z|-peaks by **contemporaneous spread-widening / depth-depletion**; measure forward **retracement fraction** and **path gappiness** (jump count). **Prediction:** vacuum episodes are gappy, fast, near-fully retracing, and depth-collapse-coincident; non-vacuum episodes are not. **Falsified if** retracement is independent of depth or episodes are smooth/non-reverting. *Gate:* requires order-book depth / spread (not in any current file — OHLC only; doc 12 §3 data). A *price-only* proxy stub — partition by intrabar range-spike / overnight-gap and test forward retracement on the volume-bearing subset — is the only near-term observational shadow; flagged as weak (range ≠ depth).

## 6. Trend overshoot / extrapolation (overreaction)

**(a) Mechanism.** Extrapolative demand: investors weight **recent past price changes** as a forecast of future changes, buying winners *because* they rose; demand chases return, pushing price **beyond** fundamental value, and "**waver**" between extrapolation and a value anchor sustains then bursts the overshoot (Barberis–Greenwood–Jin–Shleifer 2018, *Extrapolation and Bubbles*, JFE; 2015 X-CAPM, JFE). De Bondt–Thaler (1985) is the reduced-form long-horizon consequence: 3–5-year extreme winners/losers **reverse**.

**(b) Why continuation around large deviations.** Extrapolation is a **positive-feedback** mechanism: a large deviation *recruits* extrapolative demand, which extends the move (continuation) and lifts it **further from equilibrium** — the deviation feeds itself. Distinct from information momentum (§3): the continuation is **un-anchored to fundamentals** (driven by past returns, not news) and is followed — at **long horizon** — by reversal (De Bondt–Thaler), making it a **slow-transitory** mechanism (eventually reverts, but not on a microstructure clock). Distinct from spirals (§4): driven by *belief/demand*, not forced deleveraging, and accompanied by **high and rising volume** (Barberis et al.: bubbles have high volume increasing with past returns).

**(c) Separating signature vs the null & rivals.** Fingerprints the null lacks: (i) **continuation strength increasing with the length/consistency of the prior run** (extrapolation feeds on *past returns*, so multi-period trailing-return-conditioned continuation exceeds |z|-conditioned continuation) — a George–Hwang-style nearness-to-extreme conditioning that the symmetric |z| partition does not capture; (ii) **rising volume with the deviation** (Barberis et al.), a flow co-signature; (iii) **long-horizon reversal** (De Bondt–Thaler 3–5y) — separating it from the *permanent* repricing of §3 and the *fast* reversal of §2/§4/§5; (iv) **excess kurtosis / overshoot past a value anchor** (deviation overshoots then mean-reverts on a slow clock). Versus the null: selection gives continuation with **no horizon-dependent reversal and no volume gradient**; extrapolation gives slow-reverting, volume-accompanied, past-return-graded continuation.

**(d) Future falsification hook (cheap, causal, pre-registerable — partly runnable).** Pre-register a **horizon-curve reversal** test (return-space, smoother-safe): condition continuation episodes on **trailing multi-period return magnitude** (not just terminal |z|), and measure the **variance-ratio / autocorrelation curve over horizon q** — extrapolation predicts **VR>1 at short q** (continuation) crossing to **VR<1 at long q** (reversal), with the crossover deeper for longer prior runs (Lo–MacKinlay VR is inherently multi-horizon; doc 12 §3 endorses VR-as-curve-over-horizon as *characterisation*). **Prediction:** a short-continuation / long-reversal sign flip, monotone in prior-run length, **absent** in a matched RW/OU surrogate. **Falsified if** VR is monotone (no flip) or the flip matches the surrogate. *Note:* the horizon-VR-curve leg is **runnable now** on the trusted cohort (price-only, the licensed multi-horizon characterisation); the *volume-gradient* leg is data-gated. This is the cleanest **price-only** separator from the null in the whole map.

## 7. Volatility clustering (GARCH) masquerading as continuation

**(a) Mechanism.** Returns are conditionally heteroskedastic — large moves cluster (GARCH-type variance persistence; Cont 2001 stylized facts). There is **no mean predictability**, only **variance** persistence. But conditioning a *level* window on |z| ≥ θ preferentially samples **high-variance regimes** (large |z| needs large recent innovations), and in a high-variance cluster a directional run is *larger in magnitude*, which **inflates dir_eff and biases ACF estimators** — a purely second-moment phenomenon read as first-moment continuation. Lobato–Savin (1998) / Lobato–Nankervis–Savin (2001): under GARCH dependence the sample-autocorrelation covariance is **not** identity, so standard ACF/Q tests give **misleading inference** — apparent return autocorrelation that is an artifact of conditional heteroskedasticity.

**(b) Why continuation around large deviations.** Selecting on |z| selects on a volatility cluster; within the cluster, paths are *bigger* and naive directional/persistence descriptors are *biased upward* even with zero true drift. So GARCH manufactures **apparent** continuation around large deviations **without any mean dynamic at all** — it is a *measurement-class* confound, the second-moment sibling of the selection null.

**(c) Separating signature vs the null & rivals — and a warning.** This mechanism is **least separable**: it is essentially the **null's variance channel**, and a properly *matched* surrogate in §1 **should already be GARCH-calibrated** to neutralize it. Its distinguishing mark is **negative**: continuation that **vanishes once the surrogate carries the real conditional variance** (i.e., real ≈ GARCH-surrogate even where it exceeded an RW-surrogate) → the "continuation" was variance clustering, not a mean mechanism. It carries **no** post-peak reversal (§2/§4/§5), **no** μ\*-break (§3), **no** long-horizon flip (§6), **no** cross-sectional flow structure. Positively, it predicts dir_eff/ACF inflation **tracking realized-volatility percentile** of the window and **disappearing under variance-standardized returns** (divide each window's returns by a causal conditional-σ estimate before computing dir_eff/ACF).

**(d) Future falsification hook (cheap, causal, pre-registerable — runnable now).** Pre-register two checks: (i) **variance-standardization** — recompute dir_eff and ACF1 on **causally-devolatilized** returns (returns ÷ causal EGARCH/rolling-σ); **prediction:** continuation **attenuates substantially** if it was GARCH-driven, **survives** if it is a true mean mechanism. (ii) **GARCH-matched surrogate** — the §1 surrogate calibrated as GARCH(1,1) to each instrument; **prediction:** real ≈ GARCH-surrogate ⇒ attribute to clustering. **Falsified as a primary explanation if** continuation **survives** devolatilization and **exceeds** the GARCH surrogate. *Both legs runnable now* (price-only; ARCH/`arch` is in the frozen stack §8). This check is **mandatory hygiene** before crediting *any* §2–§6 mechanism — it is the second-moment half of the null.

## 8. Permanent vs transitory: the decomposition AMR actually cares about

AMR's terminal interest is **reversion**, not continuation per se. Continuation is only interesting insofar as it is **transitory** (a deviation that will revert) rather than **permanent** (a repricing that will not). This section maps the *distinction*, not a claim — it is the axis along which the §2–§7 mechanisms sort.

**The decomposition (classical).** A price is modelled as **permanent (random-walk / martingale) component + transitory (stationary, mean-reverting) component** (Beveridge–Nelson 1981 trend–cycle decomposition; Fama–French 1988 *Permanent and Temporary Components of Stock Prices*, JPE — a slowly-decaying stationary component generates **negative long-horizon autocorrelation**). Muth/Watson state-space and the Kalman/Beveridge–Nelson identity make this operational: the **permanent** part is the long-run forecast level (where μ\* settles); the **transitory** part is the mean-reverting gap. **Continuation that eventually reverts = a large transitory component still building or overshooting; continuation that permanently reprices = the permanent component itself moving (μ\* relocates).**

**How the mechanisms sort on this axis (the payoff of the whole map):**

| Mechanism | Transitory vs permanent | Reversal timescale | μ\* behaviour | Cleanest separating observable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| §1 Selection-on-deviation (NULL) | neither (artifact) | n/a (no true dynamic) | stationary | real ≈ matched surrogate |
| §2 Inventory / risk-transfer | **transitory** | fast (~1 day; HM 0.92d) | stable, returns | flow-graded fast post-peak reversal |
| §3 Information momentum | **permanent** | none (no reversal) | **relocates** | μ\*-break + news-conditioned, no reversal |
| §4 Forced positioning / spiral | **transitory** | medium (days) | stable, returns | convex + cross-asset + post-exhaustion reversal |
| §5 Liquidity vacuum | **transitory** | fastest (intraday V) | stable, returns | gappy + depth-collapse + ~full retrace |
| §6 Trend extrapolation | **transitory (slow)** | long (DBT 3–5y) | overshoots then returns | horizon-VR sign flip, run-length-graded |
| §7 GARCH clustering | neither (variance artifact) | n/a | stable | vanishes under devolatilization |

**The single discriminator that does the most work (validated, inherited).** Per doc 12 §2 / doc 08 §7, the trustworthy axis is **equilibrium stability** — "**did μ\* stay put?**" — *not* "did price come back?" (the latter is smoother-manufactured). On the permanent/transitory axis this is exactly right: **transitory mechanisms (§2,§4,§5,§6) leave μ\* eventually intact; the permanent mechanism (§3) moves μ\*.** A return-space **variance-ratio curve over horizon q** (Lo–MacKinlay; doc 12 §3) is the one smoother-trap-safe statistic that reads this directly: **VR<1 at the reversion horizon ⇒ transitory; VR≈1 (martingale) at all horizons ⇒ permanent.** This is the bridge from "continuation" (the surviving finding) back to "reversion" (what AMR is for) — and it is **price-only and runnable now** on a trusted, verified-MR instrument.

**Non-claims (explicit).** (i) This map asserts *no* mechanism is operative in the deployment domain — that is untested and the cohort's MR character is unverified (doc 11 non-conclusion 3; doc 12 §2). (ii) The mechanisms are **not mutually exclusive** — a single large-deviation episode can mix repricing + inventory + clustering; real attribution is a *future* mixed-mechanism problem, not a single-label classification. (iii) None of this licenses a detector, a per-bar score, or State-T timing (doc 11 §6 FROZEN). (iv) Several falsification hooks are **data-gated** (flow/OI/depth absent) and are specified for a *future* data-acquisition state (doc 12 Arm D unlock), not for execution now.

## 9. Ranking: how cleanly each mechanism separates from the selection-on-deviation null

Ranked by **separability** = does the mechanism predict a *specific, signed, surrogate-exceeding* real residual the null cannot produce, **and** can that be tested *cheaply, causally, price-only, now*?

| Rank | Mechanism | Separation from null | Testable now (price-only)? | Why it ranks here |
